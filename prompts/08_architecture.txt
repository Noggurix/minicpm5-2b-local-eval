You are designing a payment worker.

Constraints:

- PostgreSQL is the only database.
- Jobs are delivered at least once.
- Two or more workers may process jobs concurrently.
- Workers may crash at any instruction.
- The external payment provider supports an idempotency key.
- There are no distributed transactions between PostgreSQL and the payment provider.
- Retrying a job must never intentionally charge the customer twice.

Design the processing algorithm.

Explain:
- how the database state is modeled;
- how concurrent workers are controlled;
- where the idempotency key comes from;
- what happens if a worker crashes immediately after the provider accepts the charge but before PostgreSQL records success;
- whether the whole system can truthfully claim exactly-once execution.

Keep the answer under 350 words.
