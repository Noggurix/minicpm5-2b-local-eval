There are two identical workers. Each worker can execute only one task at a time.
Tasks are non-preemptive.

Durations and dependencies:

A: 4 minutes, no dependencies
B: 3 minutes, no dependencies
C: 5 minutes, depends on A
D: 2 minutes, depends on A and B
E: 4 minutes, depends on B
F: 3 minutes, depends on C and D

Find the minimum possible makespan.

Give:
1. the minimum completion time;
2. one valid optimal schedule;
3. a short argument showing why no faster schedule is possible.
